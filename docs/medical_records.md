# Medical Records and Privacy — Lakeside Family Clinic (fictional sample data)

## Requesting your records
Patients can download visit summaries and test results from the patient portal at no cost.
Requests for a complete copy of your medical record must be made in writing and are fulfilled within 30 days.

## Sharing records with someone else
Records are only released to a third party with the patient's written consent.
Parents or guardians can access records for children under 16.

## Privacy
Clinic staff only access a patient's record when it is needed for that patient's care or administration.
All access to patient records is logged and audited.
