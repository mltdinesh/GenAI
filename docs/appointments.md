# Appointments Policy — Lakeside Family Clinic (fictional sample data)

## Booking
Patients can book appointments through the patient portal, by phone, or at the front desk.
General Medicine and Paediatrics accept same-day bookings when slots are available.
Cardiology and Dermatology require a referral from a General Medicine doctor before the first visit.

## Cancellations
Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment are recorded as a late cancellation.
Three late cancellations or missed appointments within six months may require future bookings to be made in person.

## Late arrival
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to rebook so that other patients are not delayed.

## Telehealth
Follow-up consultations in General Medicine can be held by video call.
First visits to a specialist must be in person.
