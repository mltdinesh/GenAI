# Prescription Refills — Lakeside Family Clinic (fictional sample data)

## Requesting a refill
Refill requests can be submitted through the patient portal or by asking your pharmacy to contact the clinic.
Please allow 3 business days for a refill request to be processed.

## When a visit is required
Controlled medications cannot be refilled without an appointment with the prescribing doctor.
If you have not seen your doctor in the last 12 months, a review appointment is required before a refill is approved.

## Urgent situations
If you will run out of an essential medication before a refill can be processed, call the clinic directly.
The clinic does not handle emergencies; in an emergency, contact emergency services.
